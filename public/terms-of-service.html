  • ការបណ្តុះបណ្តាលវិជ្ជាជីវៈ៖ វគ្គបណ្តុះបណ្តាលអនឡាញ និងក្រៅបណ្តាញសម្រាប់ការអភិវឌ្ឍជំនាញ

  • ការវាយតម្លៃឥណទាន៖ ការណែនាំឥណទានប៉ុណ្ណោះ ដែលផ្តល់ដោយដៃគូដែលមានអាជ្ញាបណ្ណ

1服务概述

高棉职通车（Khmer Career Express）是连接柬埔寨求职者、雇主和培训机构的数字平台。我们的服务包括：

  • 职位发布与简历匹配：为雇主提供职位发布平台，为求职者提供申请渠道
  • 职业培训：线上和线下技能发展培训课程
  • 支付服务：仅用于平台服务费用的支付处理（非贷款服务）
  • 信用评估参考：仅提供信贷建议，由持牌合作机构提供实际服务
⚖️
法律框架：本条款受柬埔寨王国宪法和商业法管辖。使用我们的服务即表示您已阅读、理解并同意遵守这些条款。

1Service Overview

Khmer Career Express is a digital platform connecting job seekers, employers, and training institutes in Cambodia. Our services include:

  • Job Posting & Resume Matching: A platform for employers to post jobs and for job seekers to apply
  • Professional Training: Online and offline skill development courses
  • Payment Services: Payment processing for platform service fees only (not lending)
  • Credit Assessment: Credit referral only, actual services provided by licensed partners
⚖️
Legal Framework: These terms are governed by the Constitution and Commercial Law of the Kingdom of Cambodia. Using our services indicates that you have read, understood, and agreed to comply with these terms.

您的账户仅限个人使用，不得转让、出售或出租给他人。严禁与他人共用账户。

2User Accounts

2.2 Account Security

  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password

2.3 Account Transfer Prohibited

Your account is personal and may not be transferred, sold, or leased. Account sharing is strictly prohibited.

3求职者条款

3.1 简历真实性

  • 您必须确保简历中的信息真实准确
  • 禁止伪造学历、工作经验或证书
  • 您对您发布的所有信息负责
  • 包含不当内容的简历将被立即删除

3.2 申请行为

  • 仅申请您真正感兴趣的职位
  • 不要使用不同简历重复申请同一职位
  • 不得将服务用于非法就业目的

3.3 面试安排

  • 请按时参加面试
  • 如无法参加，请至少提前24小时通知雇主
  • 我们不对面试过程中雇主的行为负责

💡 求职者提示

面试后请通过社交媒体核查公司背景，并仔细阅读职位描述以识别虚假招聘的警示信号。

3Job Seeker Terms

3.1 Resume Truthfulness

  • You must ensure all information in your resume is true and accurate
  • Falsifying education, work experience, or certifications is prohibited
  • You are responsible for all information you post
  • Resumes with inappropriate content will be removed immediately

3.2 Application Conduct

  • Only apply for jobs you are genuinely interested in
  • Do not submit multiple applications with different resumes for the same position
  • The service must not be used for illegal employment purposes

3.3 Interview Arrangements

  • Attend interviews at the scheduled time
  • If unable to attend, notify the employer at least 24 hours in advance
  • We are not responsible for employer conduct during interviews

💡 Tips for Job Seekers

After interviews, verify companies through social media and carefully read job descriptions to identify warning signs of fake recruitment.

10Dispute Resolution

10.1 Governing Law

⚖️

10.2 Dispute Resolution Procedure

In case of a dispute, both parties must follow these steps:

  • Step 1 - Negotiation: Both parties shall first attempt to resolve the dispute through direct negotiation within 30 days
  • Step 2 - Legal Action: If negotiation fails, either party may file a lawsuit with Cambodian courts

Disputes involving international companies may be submitted to the ICC Arbitration Center in Singapore or Paris.

10.4 Jurisdiction
